Use the writer agent with skill paper-playbook for section $ARGUMENTS. For a new or substantially restructured section, inspect the relevant reference passages and record their content, function and information dependencies, then make a compact section plan before drafting. Follow the actual results and shared-usage terminology rather than a fixed paragraph count, an invented technique name or a mandatory compiler pipeline. Reuse an existing plan for small corrections and continue through the authorized rewrite without an extra planning approval.

Only use survived claims from CLAIMS.md (weakened claims with their weakened wording) and own numerical results present in EVIDENCE.md. State model, notion and assumptions as in the zk-definitions assumption ledger in THEORY.md. Distinguish theoretical parameters from implemented configurations using THEORY.md, CIRCUITS.md and impl/ as applicable. Preserve hypotheses and comparison boundaries. Run crbench audit-tex after manuscript numerical edits/builds and check affected references and rendered pages. A skills-only update does not authorize a manuscript rewrite or require a paper build.

Apply paper-playbook/references/clear-exposition.md throughout: one main question per sentence unless the combined points are simple, explicit A → B → C transitions, and an explanation of the actual reduction before its implementation obstacles. Keep standard Fiat–Shamir and commitment-before-challenge steps out of contribution headlines. Describe the interactive protocol first when appropriate, then give the justified noninteractive transformation briefly, retaining exact transcript and security requirements in their proper specification.
