\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

On one toy backend, lookups beat bit decomposition for range checks by about four times in prover time once the
range is wide enough to amortise the table, gain nothing for narrow ranges, and never reach the five times we first
claimed. The two bugs we planted and found are the useful part for practitioners: an exhaustive small-field
audit of the real circuit builder catches what random tests cannot, and every sub-protocol transcript must be
tested with the prover's messages held fixed. Real systems with table-independent lookups~\cite{EFG22} or
decomposable tables~\cite{STW24} change the constants, not the shape of this trade-off.
